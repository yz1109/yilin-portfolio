Mobile app UX Design Academic project 2024

Cultural Buddy Finder

Helping international students find peers who share their cultural traditions — so they can plan small celebrations, stay connected to home, and share meaningful moments while studying abroad.

My role
UX Designer
Team
Yilin Zhu & Julia Zheng
Context
Cornell University — academic project
Problem statement

The challenge

International students often feel disconnected from their cultural traditions while studying abroad. Academic schedules conflict with holidays, and finding others to celebrate with can be difficult — especially without an existing social network in a new country.

"How might we help international students find peers who share their traditions, so they can celebrate meaningfully — even far from home?"

User research

Three key challenges

1
Disconnection during cultural holidays
Students struggle to recreate meaningful traditions abroad without family, familiar foods, or community context.
2
Coordination friction
Planning even small celebrations requires significant effort across schedules and locations — most students give up before even starting.
3
Lack of structured support in existing apps
General social media platforms don't support culturally-focused matching or celebration planning in any structured way.
Research insights

What users told us

Users want clear continuity between planning and reflecting — the app should feel like one journey, not disconnected screens.
They expect a visible history of events and saved content — memory and reflection matter as much as planning.
Matching must feel transparent and trustworthy — users won't connect with strangers unless they understand why they're being matched.
Features that duplicate existing social apps must provide clear added value — otherwise users will just use Instagram.
Persona

Who we designed for

👩‍🎓
Aera Kang
Cornell international student · Seoul, South Korea
  • Find other students who share her cultural traditions
  • Plan small celebrations that fit into a busy academic schedule
  • Feel emotionally connected to her home culture
  • Share meaningful moments with family abroad
Aera doesn't need a large event-planning platform. She needs a simple, emotionally supportive tool that helps her connect, celebrate briefly, and reflect meaningfully.
Design goals

Our north stars

🌏
Meaningful connection
Help international students find peers who genuinely share their traditions — not just their country of origin.
🔒
Clarity and trust
Provide clear feedback and system status in every interaction so users always know what's happening.
⚡
Reduce friction
Make organizing small celebrations quick and simple — three taps, not thirty minutes.
📖
Continuity & reflection
Allow users to revisit past celebrations and share meaningful recaps with people back home.
